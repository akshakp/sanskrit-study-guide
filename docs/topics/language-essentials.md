# Language Essentials (लघु-प्रवेशः)

<div id="pageTabbedStudy" hidden></div>

The alphabet, pronunciation places, and the core language concepts of Sanskrit,
consolidated into one page.


=== "Alphabets (वर्णमाला)"

    ## Vowels (स्वर)

    A **स्वर** is a sound that can be pronounced independently, without help from another sound. Sanskrit traditionally lists 9 basic vowels.

    | Type                 | Vowels                                    | Mātrā / Time |
    | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------ |
    | **ह्रस्व / Short**   | अ इ उ ऋ लृ (A I U Ru Lru)                 | 1 mātrā      |
    | **दीर्घ / Long**     | [आ ई ऊ ॠ] ए ऐ ओ औ (ā EE OO Roo) E Ai O Au | 2 mātrā      |
    | **प्लुत / Prolated** | अ३ इ३ उ३ ऋ३ ऌ३ ए३ ऐ३ ओ३ औ३                | 3 mātrā      |

    ---

    ## Consonants (व्यञ्जन)

    A consonant is pronounced with the help of a vowel before or after. Total count: **33**.

    | Group               | Aghoṣa (Voiceless) | Ghoṣa (Voiced)        |
    | ------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------- |
    | **क / कु (Ka/Ku)**  | क् ख् (K Kh)       | ग् घ् ङ् (G Gh Gn)    |
    | **च / चु (Ch/Chu)** | च् छ् (Ch Chh)     | ज् झ् ञ् (J Jh Yn)    |
    | **ट / टु (T/Tu)**   | ट् ठ् (T Th)       | ड् ढ् ण् (D Dh N)     |
    | **त / तु (T/Tu)**   | त् थ् (T Th)       | द् ध् न् (D Dh N)     |
    | **प / पु (P/Ph)**   | प् फ् (P Ph)       | ब् भ् म् (B Bh M)     |
    | -                   | —                  | य् र् ल् व् (Y R L V) |
    | -                   | श् ष् स् (Sh Sh S) | —                     |
    | -                   | —                  | ह् (H)                |

    | Place (स्थान)                  | Consonants               | Vowels |
    | ------------------------------ | ------------------------ | ------ |
    | **कण्ठ (Throat)**              | क् ख् ग् घ् **ङ्** ह्    | अ आ    |
    | **तालु (Palate)**              | च् छ् ज् झ् **ञ्** श् य् | इ ई    |
    | **मूर्धा (Retroflex)**         | ट् ठ् ड् ढ् **ण्** ष् र् | ऋ ॠ    |
    | **दन्त (Teeth)**               | त् थ् द् ध् **न्** स् ल् | लृ     |
    | **ओष्ठ (Lips)**                | प् फ् ब् भ् **म्**       | उ ऊ    |
    | **कण्ठतालु (Throat + Palate)** | —                        | ए ऐ    |
    | **कण्ठोष्ठ (Throat + Lips)**   | —                        | ओ औ    |

    !!! tip "Anunāsika (अनुनासिक)"
        Letters marked in **bold** in the original notes are called **Anunāsika** because they use the nostrils for pronunciation.


=== "Language Concepts"

    ## Five Parts of a Sentence

    A Sanskrit sentence is built from meaningful word types. The two most essential parts are **noun** and **verb**.

    | Sanskrit | English | Function | Example |
    |----------|---------|----------|---------|
    | **[संज्ञा / नाम](nouns.md)** | Noun | Names a person, place, thing, or idea | रामः, पुस्तकम् |
    | **[क्रियापदम्](verbs.md)** | Verb | Shows action, occurrence, or state | पठति, गच्छति |
    | **[विशेषणम्](adjectives.md)** | Adjective | Describes a noun | सुन्दरः, स्वस्थः |
    | **[सर्वनाम](#core-pronouns)** | Pronoun | Stands in place of noun | सः, सा, तत् |
    | **[अव्ययम्](#indeclinables)** | Indeclinable | Adds meaning to a word or sentence without changing its form | अत्र, तत्र, च, न, अन्यत्र |

    !!! tip "Every Sanskrit word is either विकारी (Variable) or अविकारी (Invariable)"

        - **अव्ययम्** is the **only अविकारी (Invariable)** part of speech. Its form never changes.
        - **Everything else** (**संज्ञा / नाम, सर्वनाम, विशेषणम्, and क्रियापदम्**) is **विकारी (Variable)**, meaning its form changes according to gender, number, case, person, tense, etc.

    ---

    ## Gender (लिङ्ग)

    Every Sanskrit noun has grammatical gender. Adjectives and many pronouns must agree with the noun.

    | Gender | Sanskrit | Example | Meaning |
    |--------|----------|---------|---------|
    | Masculine | **पुल्लिङ्ग** | बालकः | boy |
    | Feminine | **स्त्रीलिङ्ग** | बालिका | girl |
    | Neuter | **नपुंसकलिङ्ग** | पुस्तकम् | book |

    ---

    ## Number (वचन)

    | वचन | Meaning | Example |
    |------|---------|---------|
    | **एकवचन** | One | देवः |
    | **द्विवचन** | Two | देवौ |
    | **बहुवचन** | More than two | देवाः |

    ---

    ## Person (पुरुष)

    !!! note "पुरुष means a participant in communication, not male person."

    | पुरुष | Role in Communication | English Equivalent |
    |-------|----------------------|-------------------|
    | **प्रथम** | Others: he, she, it, they | Third person |
    | **मध्यम** | Listener: you | Second person |
    | **उत्तम** | Speaker: I, we | First person |

    ---

    ## Core Pronouns (सर्वनाम)

    | Person / Gender | Singular | Dual | Plural |
    |----------------|----------|------|--------|
    | **प्रथम Masculine** | सः | तौ | ते |
    | **प्रथम Feminine** | सा | ते | ताः |
    | **प्रथम Neuter** | तत् | ते | तानि |
    | **मध्यम** | त्वम् | युवाम् | यूयम् |
    | **उत्तम** | अहम् | आवाम् | वयम् |

    ## Indeclinables (अव्ययम्)

    **अव्ययम्** is an indeclinable word whose form remains unchanged across all three genders (लिङ्ग), eight cases (विभक्ति), and three numbers (वचन).

    **The associated noun changes its case, but the अव्ययम् itself never changes.**

    For the complete list of words and meanings, see [Vocabulary — Adverbs & Particles](../reference/vocabulary.md#adverbs-particles).

    !!! note "द्वितीया विभक्ति with specific अव्यय"

        When the following अव्ययानि are used in a sentence, **the noun or pronoun governed by the अव्ययम्** (object) takes **द्वितीया विभक्ति**.

         **उभयतः · परितः · सर्वतः · विना · अन्तरेण · ऋते · अन्तरा · प्रति · धिक्**

        अहं **मन्दिरं प्रति** गच्छामि। (*I go towards the temple.*)

        - With प्रति **मन्दिरं** → द्वितीया विभक्ति is used and not  **मन्दिरस्य** → षष्ठी विभक्ति
